Plugin one · ui

Interfaces that look like your product, not a template.

Four design skills, 24 Markdown design guides and two small Node scripts for fonts, icons, component kits and design-system documentation. Your brief, screens and tokens come first.

◆deck-designDesign that looks like your product◆deck-reviewCritique or gate a built interface◆deck-radarEvidence from real design systems◆deck-matchScreenshot to a 1:1 working UI

deck-design

Design, build, redesign or polish web, iOS and Android interfaces.

You sayRedesign our billing settings page so the current plan and invoices are easy to scan.
  • Reads your existing screens, components and tokens before drawing anything
  • Picks the right playbook: new work, polish, bolder, quieter, typeset, layout…
  • Renders at 375, 768 and 1440 px and fixes what it sees

deck-review

Critique, audit or gate a built interface before it ships.

You sayReview the onboarding flow before we ship. Is it ready, and does it look generic?
  • Judges the build against its brief, not against taste
  • Catches template-looking UI and accessibility gaps
  • Read-only unless you ask for fixes

deck-radar

Answer one UI question with evidence from established design systems.

You sayHow do design systems handle a destructive confirmation dialog?
  • Searches about 550 pages from 26 design systems, offline
  • Material, Apple HIG, Polaris, WAI-ARIA, USWDS, GOV.UK and more
  • Returns one structural lesson, then gets back to your task

deck-match

Recreate a screenshot, mockup or design export as a 1:1 working UI.

You sayBuild this screenshot as a React page and match it exactly.
  • Measures regions, spacing and type scale from the image
  • Renders, compares side by side and corrects
  • Keeps text and controls real, never a pasted screenshot

Plugin two · audio

From a script to finished audio, free and on your machine.

Narration, dialogue, audiobooks, lessons, podcasts, drama, ads and meditations. Ten voices, licensed sound, a transcription QA gate, mastering and captions. No account, no API key, no per-character bill.

♫deck-scriptScripts written for the ear♫deck-voiceOn-device voices, QA and captions♫deck-mixLicensed sound, cues and mastering

deck-script

Write scripts for the ear: narration, lessons, podcasts, drama, ads, meditations and kids' stories.

You sayTurn this article into a 60-second narration for a calm female voice.
  • Writes a plain .audio.md script with cast, delivery and cues
  • Phrasing that carries emotion, not just tags

deck-voice

Render on-device with Supertonic 3: casting, 14 delivery presets, QA, mix, captions, and your own voice.

You sayMake an audiobook of chapter one with a different voice for Holmes.
  • Every line transcribed and scored; failures re-rendered
  • Hits its loudness target, ACX included, or stops with the numbers

deck-mix

Find licensed sound effects, ambience and music, place cues, duck, remix and re-master a rendered piece.

You sayAdd rain behind the second scene and duck the music under the voices.
  • Openverse, Wikimedia Commons and Freesound, filtered to CC0 and CC BY, each credited
  • Remixes from the timeline and stems instead of re-rendering the voices
lighthouse/script.audio.md
---
title: The Lighthouse
preset: drama

For your Mac · Keepwatch

Keep your Mac awake while agents run, even with the lid closed.

A free, open-source menu-bar app. It stays awake exactly while Claude Code, Codex and other agents work, runs lid-closed sessions that go back to sleep on schedule, and keeps watch on your mic and camera. Agents get a keepwatch command and a skill.

Meet Keepwatch Source on GitHub
How it is built

Small, readable and yours.

Every skill is something you can open and read. Nothing phones home.

Plain Markdown

Skills and guides are Markdown files plus small dependency-free scripts. Read them, fork them, change them.

Runs locally

Voices and QA run on your CPU after a one-time download. No account, no API key, no telemetry.

Licensed and credited

Every font, icon and sound comes with its licence; sounds are CC0 or CC BY and credited.

A growing deck

One domain per plugin. UI and audio today, with more domains on the roadmap.

Install

Two commands. Then just ask.

Inside Claude Code, add the marketplace once and install the plugins you want. Needs Node 18+; audio also needs Python 3.10+ and ffmpeg.

claude: ~/your-project
> /plugin marketplace add AgentSkillDeck/skills
> /plugin install ui@agentskilldeck
> /plugin install audio@agentskilldeck

# or one skill, with the skills CLI
$ npx skills add AgentSkillDeck/skills --skill deck-design
